## Platform

Web. Astro generates static pages for GitHub Pages. The CMS continues to edit `editorial/content/portfolio.json` and publish media through GitHub.

## Purpose

KLTZQU is Eugene’s portfolio for independent games, apps and tools. Visitors can browse projects, look at real development material and find ways to play, open, or contact the author.

## Content

Keep English and Russian copy aligned and factual. Organized collections include work, Lab and posts when published. Use existing project footage and screenshots with editorial crops, and keep works without media accessible in the project index.

## Constraints

Direct project links, browser history, search, galleries, contact details, CMS workflows and metadata remain available. The portfolio works with keyboard, touch and reduced motion, and project navigation stays available when the 3D scene falls back to still images.
